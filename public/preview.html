Our Story

Skincare
Meets Heritage.

Born from memory,
built with purpose.

Malab Skin began in a kitchen in Brussels, where our founder Amara Bah would recreate the skincare rituals she remembered from her childhood in West Africa — rich shea preparations, honey masks, and oil blends that left skin genuinely nourished.

What started as a personal practice became a professional obsession: how do you scale something so rooted in touch, memory, and place without losing what makes it special?

The answer was to start very small and grow very deliberately. Every formula begins with a single ingredient — researched, ethically sourced, tested extensively — and built out from there.

What We Stand For

Our principles,
not just our policies.

01
Ancestral Wisdom

Every ingredient traces back to a tradition. We research, we listen, and we honour what has worked for centuries before adding it to a formula.

02
Clean Formulation

No parabens, no synthetic fragrances, no filler ingredients. Every component earns its place by contributing measurably to skin health.

03
Ethical Sourcing

We partner directly with cooperatives in Ghana, Morocco, Rwanda, and across the continent — fair-trade, traceable, and community-first.

04
Made in Belgium

All formulations are developed and produced in Belgium, combining African raw materials with rigorous European cosmetic standards.

Our Ingredients

Every ingredient has a reason to be there.

We trace every raw material to its origin — working directly with farming cooperatives, small producers, and family operations across Africa. The quality you feel on your skin starts long before the formula is made.
